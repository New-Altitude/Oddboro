- [staff] **Decision (2026-11-03): what an Odd sees through a thread is no wider than its own access, unless the owner says yes.** Odds hold different tools, one grant per connector, and everyone on a thread reads its folder and its briefs, so a colleague could read what came from a tool it lacks. The office cannot see where words or files came from, so whenever work is handed on a thread it compares access with every Odd on it, the hub and each colleague handed work there, in one place (`lacking`). Any grant an Odd holds counts as holding the tool, since what it read can sit in its notes; only a grant its runs get now counts as the reader's own. The office never holds a hand-off for it: a hub whose work needs a tool's contents the colleague lacks is told to put that to the owner with `raise_ask` first and hand off after the yes, which reaches it as any ask's answer does. Each tool others on the thread have that the colleague lacks is listed in the colleague's brief, so it does not expect its contents. A colleague holding a tool someone on the thread lacks is told in its brief never to write that tool's contents into its part of the folder or its reply, and to put it to the owner with `raise_ask` if the work needs it. The owner ruled on 2026-11-03 to build it, and on 2026-11-04 to drop the `share` ask that held a hand-off for their yes, which supersedes the 2026-11-03 ruling. Code in `packages/ledger/src/sharing.ts` (`lacking`, `sharing`), `packages/ledger/src/hand-offs.ts` (`handOff`, `handOffBrief`, `handOffContext`), `packages/ledger/src/lead-brief.ts` (`threadBrief`) and `packages/ledger/src/mcp.ts` (`hand_off`).
