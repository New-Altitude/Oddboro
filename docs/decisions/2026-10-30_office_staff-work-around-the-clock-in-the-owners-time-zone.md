- [office] **Decision (2026-10-30): the office keeps the owner's time zone, which setup asks for and writes to `.env` as `OFFICE_TIME_ZONE` (this computer's own zone until set). Staff work around the clock, and jobs run on one timer set for the soonest due job, under a login service that keeps the computer awake. Every run is told the scheduled time it is for, and a job whose time passed while the office was off follows its kind, `latest` or `record` (ruled 2026-11-11).** The office is where its owner is, not where the screen is, and agents do not sleep. One timer is the least work that keeps a schedule. Each run records whether its schedule or the owner started it, and a manual run never moves the schedule; Run now is for the moment it is pressed. A run counts and labels its results by the time it is for, never by when it started. A `latest` job (the default: sweeps, reports, reminders) catches up once if less than half a period passed since its newest missed time, else skips to its next time and is listed once on the desk's While you were away card, with Run now. A `record` job (each run adds an entry to a series) fills every missed period, found by stepping through `nextRun`, in one run that lists them oldest first and writes "no data" for a period it cannot fetch; past 10 periods the office asks the owner first, one ask per job. The Odd setting up a job picks its kind, and the owner can change it on the job. Catch-ups start one after another, never together, and a job on time still starts at once. This supersedes the 2026-10-30 ruling that every missed job runs once when the clock next looks: after a week away that was a burst of runs, many pointless, and a series lost its missed days.
