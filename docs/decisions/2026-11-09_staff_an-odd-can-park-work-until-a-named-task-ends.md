- [staff] **Decision (2026-11-09): a lead can park work until a named task ends, and the task ending wakes it.** Work that waits on something the owner does, such as a task on their desk, had only a time to wait for, so the lead woke early or sat idle. The lead parks the work with `resume_at` and its `after_task`, the number of a task on other work that the lead gave or that is on work it is on, which sets the column `resume_on_task` on the thread and clears any time it was parked on; parking on a time with `resume_at` clears the task. Any other task reads as no task at all, so a park learns nothing of another Odd's task. When the task ends, `wakeParked` clears the park and wakes the lead once, telling it the task was finished or dropped and the owner's note. A dropped task wakes it too, and says it will not be finished, so the lead decides what comes of the work. A task taken off the desk (`withdrawTask`), as its Odd leaves the office or through its `withdraw` tool, wakes it as a drop does, and says the task was taken off the desk. A run on the task's own work picks up in the same moment (`OWN_RUN` says which ends start one), so each side is told of the other, or both do the parked work: that run names each parked work that picked up on its own and says to leave it to that work (`follow_ups.picked_up` on a `task_done` or `carry_out` run, or the answer that goes back on a drop, never the owner's note), and each parked work is told that run carries its own part. A task already on that work needs no park, since its answer already brings the lead back, and the tool says so. Stopping the work clears its park, whether the owner stops it or its lead leaves the office, and parked work is never told that a run of a lead that left, or of stopped work, carries its part. A park, on a task or a time, also ends when the lead's next run on the work starts, whatever starts it (a hand-back, the owner's words, an answer), so a task that never ends cannot hold or later wake work already picked up: that run is told the wait ended and may park again. When that run fails having parked nothing again, the park comes back, on its task or its time, so a run cut off by a sign-in or a restart loses no wait: a task that ended during the run wakes the work at once, saying it was taken off the desk when the office or its Odd took it off (`tasks.withdrawn`), even if that Odd left and came back since, and dropped when the owner dropped it, and a time that passed leaves it for the clock to wake once. A colleague's run does not end it, nor does a pick-up that waits on the lead's run still going, and a usage-window hold is the office's, so it stays. The owner ruled this on 2026-11-09, and it extends the 2026-11-03 ruling that a lead parks work until a time.
