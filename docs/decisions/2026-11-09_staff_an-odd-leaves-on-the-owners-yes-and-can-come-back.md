Nothing wakes it after, and work on its stopped threads ends with no report on the desk. A colleague's result for it, coming back after it left or waiting when it left, goes back to no one: the Ledger says the colleague finished, the lead has left and nothing picks the work up, what the colleague did stays in the work's folder, nothing goes on the desk, and the colleague pokes the owner that it finished and could not hand the work back because the lead is no longer here (the owner ruled this on 2026-11-11). Coming back turns on the jobs that were on. The manager never leaves, since every office keeps one.
