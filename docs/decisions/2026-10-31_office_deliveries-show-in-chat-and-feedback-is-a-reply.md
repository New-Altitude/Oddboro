- [office] **Decision (2026-10-31): everything a staffer puts on the desk shows in its chat, the staffer panel opens on Chat, and feedback on a report is a reply in that chat instead of a form on the desk.** The inputs sit together on the end side, and the owner answers a staffer about what it delivered in the chat where they already talk. Opening an item on the desk selects it in the sender's chat; Reply under it sends the next message with the item in full. A reply about a report marks it read and records the owner's words in the ledger as feedback, so staff still find how the owner judged it. The staffer answers in the chat run rather than a follow-up, and raises an ask for anything that needs a yes. Supersedes report feedback in the 2026-10-30 follow-up ruling. Code in `apps/office/src/lib/ui/ChatPanel.svelte` and `apps/office/src/lib/server/staff.ts` (`chatLog`) and `chat-delivery.ts` (`replyBrief`).
