- [art] **Decision (2026-11-05): every new hire gets a look of their own (except an Odd added from a New Altitude persona, whose look is the package's): a body built from face, hairstyle, top and bottom parts in their own colours, and a portrait, either drawn by the office from the owner's words or a headshot, or built by the owner from parts with a portrait made from the same parts; only Claude Code on Opus or Fable and Codex on Sol or Astra draw.** The owner can give a headshot instead of, or beside, the description; the photo goes to that one drawing run and is never stored. A drawing from words runs at low effort and takes one to two minutes; a headshot takes two medium runs, the second comparing the drawing with the photo, and five to seven minutes, which the hire window says up front (2026-10-31). A beard may take a hair colour of its own, apart from the hair. Each skin swatch carries its own lip tone, lip shade and blush, so a portrait's mouth is drawn in lip colours, never ink. No two staff may share a look, the owner sees the portrait and the walk before keeping it, and any staffer can be redrawn later, except an Odd added from a New Altitude persona. Bodies stay parts-based because 57 frames drawn per person would come out inconsistent; parts move with per-frame anchors and paint only the regions their kind may. Marshall keeps his hand-drawn art until he is redrawn. Drawing from Grok, Sonnet and the rest took past the five-minute limit or came out poorly (2026-11-05), so those offices build from parts only (`drawsLooks`); anyone may build instead of drawing, to save the time and tokens. Building uses the parts picker from the town arrival (`OddParts.svelte`), and its portrait is the sprite's head and shoulders at 3x (`partsPortrait`). A face is optional (Soft: narrower chin, blush and lips; Strong: brows and a broad chin; none: the body's own), so looks can read more feminine or more masculine, and without a beard the jaw is plain skin, since shading it read as stubble on everyone; it supersedes the 2026-10-30 ruling. Format in `packages/sprites/FORMAT.md`, code in `packages/sprites/src/look.ts` and `apps/office/src/lib/server/looks.ts`.
