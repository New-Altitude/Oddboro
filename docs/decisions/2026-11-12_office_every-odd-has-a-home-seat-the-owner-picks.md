- [office] **Decision (2026-11-12): every Odd has a home: the room it works in (`staff.room`) and, when the owner picks one, its own seat there (`staff.seat`, a floor spot id). It works at that seat whenever it is free, and no other Odd sits there. The hire window suggests a home from the role, with one line of why (plain rules in `apps/office/src/lib/staff/home.ts`, no run), and the owner changes it any time under Home on the Odd's info page (`PUT /api/staff/[id]/home`, one ledger line). Moving into another layout clears every seat, since seats are spots of the floor left behind.** The floor reads as a crowd unless each Odd has a place of its own, and a team the owner seats together should stay together. Seats are kept by spot id because that is what the floor walks to; a layout change gives new spots, so the room carries over as before and the seat starts fresh. Code in `packages/ledger/src/office-staff.ts` (`setHome`), `offices.ts` (`moveIn`), `apps/office/src/lib/floor/staff.ts` (`assign`) and `apps/office/src/lib/ui/HomeSection.svelte`.
