- [office] **Decision (2026-11-12): every Odd has a home: the room it works in (`staff.room`) and, when the owner picks one, its own seat there (`staff.seat`, a floor spot id). It works at that seat whenever it is free, and no other Odd sits there. The office recommends homes from each Odd's role and remit in one run as the office (`recommendHomes`, `POST /api/homes`), with one line of why, and falls back to plain rules (`suggestHome`) for any Odd the run gives no real room. The hire window's Where step shows the rules' pick at once and the office's when it answers; the Homes window lists every Odd, a New Altitude persona included as it joins, fills each row from Ask the office, and moves every changed Odd at once on Save homes (`PUT /api/homes`), so two can swap. The owner also changes one Odd's home under Home on its info page. The Where step adds a workspace, and the Homes window a workspace or a meeting room, at once and with no drawing, where the owner places it by end and row (`addRoom`, `POST /api/rooms`), with the page swapping the floor in place so open windows stay: to a described office's spec, or on Main Street, the sample, which has no spec, by first making it the owner's own office with the same rooms, names and desk counts, built by the office's rules; every Odd keeps its home by room name and seat label. A floor that hears a new home walks the Odd there at once. Moving into another layout keeps each seat it still has, in the room its Odd works in, and drops the rest.** The floor reads as a crowd unless each Odd has a place of its own, a team the owner seats together should stay together, and placing a dozen Odds by hand is a chore an office run does well. Recommending costs one run only when the owner asks or hires, and nothing moves until they say. Code in `packages/ledger/src/office-staff.ts` (`setHome`, `setHomes`), `offices.ts` (`moveIn`), `apps/office/src/lib/server/homes.ts`, `rooms.ts`, `apps/office/src/lib/staff/home.ts` and `apps/office/src/lib/ui/HomesPanel.svelte`.
