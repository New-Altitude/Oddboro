- [office] **Decision (2026-10-30): the owner describes the office in words and the office builds it. Claude writes a small spec (which rooms, how big, how many desks, the finishes and extras, from fixed lists); code in `packages/world` turns the spec into the floor, with the limits in code: 12 rooms, one office and lobby, 1 to 4 workspaces, 32 desks, 110 tiles wide, every seat reachable from the bus stop. A change to the office the owner is in saves over its layout, so its room names and seats stay, and keeps the version before, so the owner can undo the last change; a move in changes the layout the owner is in unless it asks for a new one, and one from the sample always makes a new layout (ruled 2026-11-18, after an owner's Claude sent the office again with new art and got a second layout of the same name); the owner can move back to any layout or the sample, and remove one they are not in (ruled 2026-11-17, after an owner ended up with two layouts of one name and no way to remove either, which supersedes the 2026-10-30 ruling that every layout is kept). What the library lacks, the office draws while it designs: up to 12 props, wall hangings, desk pieces, floors or wall styles per drawing, as .px in the house palette, kept with the layout and reused after.** Claude drawing tile maps directly would break walls, doors and paths in ways a check finds only after the fact. A spec over a fixed building shape always composes, so the freedom goes where it is safe: which rooms, their size, look and names, and when the owner says, the end of a row (`side`) and the row (`row`) a room stands in; unpinned rooms place as they always have, so a change keeps the rooms already there in place. What is in a room can be changed item by item, by tap in the large view or in words, as edits on the room in the spec (remove, move, add, by item id), checked like everything else and built without a Claude run. Drawn art goes through the same checks as library art, in the one palette, so it sits beside the hand-drawn pieces; a piece that fails is left out and its room falls back to the library. Only what a floor seen from above cannot show (ceiling height, sound) comes back as a list. Code in `packages/world/src/generate.ts`, `apps/office/src/lib/server/office-design.ts`, `office-art.ts`, `packages/sprites/src/art.ts` and `packages/ledger/src/offices.ts`.
