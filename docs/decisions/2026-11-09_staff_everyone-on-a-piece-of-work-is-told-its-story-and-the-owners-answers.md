- [staff] **Decision (2026-11-09): everyone on a piece of work is told its story and what the owner said on it, never the text of the asks.** A lead's chat read only its goals, so a colleague answering the owner about work it was on, or a lead asked about other work, did not know where it stood or what the owner had already said. `threadStory` builds one story per piece of work from its steps, what is still out and what the owner said (`ownerAnswers`, the same words the follow-up brief carries), and `my_tasks` reads a task's outcome the same way. A lead's chat is told its live goals and its latest other work (`workLedBrief`); a colleague's chat is told each piece of work it is on (`onStories`, `threadOnKnown`). Each story gives openings of the latest steps only, and a chat's stories together stay within one limit (`CHAT_STORIES_MAX`), so the chat's cost is bounded. The text of an ask is left out, since the owner's answer is what the owner said. An ask an Odd replaced with a new version (`askReplaced`) is left out as well, because the owner said nothing on it, and it reads REPLACED in `my_asks`. A finished task is told once. The owner ruled this on 2026-11-09, and it extends the 2026-11-03 ruling that what the owner tells a colleague reaches the lead.
