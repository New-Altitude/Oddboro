- [staff] **Decision (2026-10-31): goals are a record the owner owns, and they drive hiring. The owner sets a goal, or approves one the Office Manager heard in chat; the manager never sets one alone. Each goal gets one plan ask that the owner can approve in part: staff already here take on a part before anyone new is hired. A staffer sees only the goals it serves, and the owner can give a goal to more staff at any time. Onboarding asks nothing about goals: the manager's first chat does, and the owner can skip it.** Hiring had no link to anything the owner wanted, and every hire cost a separate ask. One plan per goal puts the whole answer on the desk at once, ticked by default, so the owner unticks what they do not want instead of approving each part. Preferring staff already here keeps the office small. Keeping goals per staffer keeps a run's prompt short and private. Every run's prompt carries the goals it serves, and staff log progress that the daily report reads: on a goal they serve, or on one whose thread they lead, so the lead planning a goal logs before any plan is approved and anyone serves it (owner, 2026-11-11). The manager reviews the goals weekly, a job made with the first goal so an office with none runs nothing.
