- [office] **Decision (2026-11-13): the Office menu and the desktop tray offer an always-on-top Office strip, with one still line per Odd and the owner's Waiting count.** The strip is a second window; the owner can close or hide the main window without closing it. It opens centred at 340 by 360, resizes, and remembers its last size and position when closed or when the app quits. A position on a removed display returns to the centre. An Odd's line opens its chat in the existing pane, and the count opens Waiting. It shares the floor's status words and the Odds list's waiting calculation. Its data changes through the existing office event stream, never a timer. The main window shares its visual activities only when their words change; the strip draws no floor or animation. Code in `apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/strip.rs` and `apps/office/src/routes/strip/+page.svelte`.
