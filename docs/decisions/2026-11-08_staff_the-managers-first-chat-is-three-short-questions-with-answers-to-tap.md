- [staff] **Decision (2026-11-08): an Office Manager's first chat is a short greeting, then three questions asked one at a time, each with three to five answers the owner taps or types over: what to help with first, how much to do on their own inside the office, and what to hear about between morning reports.** One message with three paragraph-long questions was the first thing a new owner met, and too much to read. One tap per question gets them started. A first answer outside the suggested choices goes straight to the manager so a brief can start work at once. With a suggested first answer the office asks the second and third question itself, with no run, so each answer shows the next at once; the third answer runs the manager, which reads all three in its chat and carries on as before, offering goals and keeping how-the-office-works answers as lessons. Which question waits is read from the chat's last line, so nothing new is stored. The second question opens with "Nothing leaves the office without your yes", and its answers cover only work inside it, so no tap promises sending without a yes (hard rule 4). The first answer to each is the default the office keeps until the owner says otherwise. Code in `packages/ledger/src/first-questions.ts`, `chat` in `apps/office/src/lib/server/staff.ts` and `apps/office/src/lib/ui/ChatPanel.svelte`. Supersedes the 2026-11-05 ruling.
