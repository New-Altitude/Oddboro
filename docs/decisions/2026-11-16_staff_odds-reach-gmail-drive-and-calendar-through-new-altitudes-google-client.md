- [staff] **Decision (2026-11-16): Odds reach Gmail, Google Drive and Google Calendar through tools the office serves itself, signed in with New Altitude's own Google Desktop client, on every system; New Altitude provides only the sign-in and never receives a token, a message, a file or an event.** No owner makes a Google client, and the token never leaves the office process: the tools run inside the office (`builtin` connectors, `apps/office/src/lib/server/google/`) behind the gateway, so a run gets only the gateway's token. Each connector is one Google account of one product, found by `config.google`, never by its id; the owner or an Odd's approved ask adds another as its own connector (`gmail-2`), and a sign-in to an address another connector of that product holds is not kept. Each signs in on its own: Gmail with `gmail.modify` (search, read, labels, trash and spam, drafts, send a draft; never permanent delete) and `gmail.settings.basic` (filters), Drive with `drive` (Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides; no delete or trash), Calendar with `calendar.readonly` (calendars, events, free/busy), `calendar.events` (create and change events) and `userinfo.email` (which account), never the full `calendar` scope, which can make and delete calendars. A sign-in that lacks a scope signs in afresh. Read only reaches the read tools, and everything else needs write: at Act it goes, and at Draft each call waits for the owner's yes. Replies and filters an Odd proposes name their account and wait on the desk, and the office sends a reply or creates a filter only on the owner's click, through that account; a filter only files mail, never deletes or forwards. Calendar events take what Google Calendar's editor offers (repeats and the three ways to change one, Meet, reminders, Drive links, colour, busy, visibility, optional guests); Zoom and other add-on calls cannot be made through Google's API, so Oddboro keeps them as they are. A calendar event that would make Google email anyone besides the owner (one created with guests, a change to an event with guests, a series split with guests) waits on the desk at every level, where the owner sees who gets an email and may edit it, and the office sends it with `sendUpdates=all` only on their click; a change to only the owner's reminders or colour emails nobody; from a run, Calendar calls go with `sendUpdates=none`. An event the office creates ends with "Added by Oddboro" and a link, which the owner may remove. Calendar never deletes an event or a calendar, and never accepts or declines an invite. Revoking the last grant cancels the sign-in at Google before the office forgets it, unless another connector still uses the same account, since Google ties one grant to the account and the client. The client's id is in `oauth.ts`; its secret, which Google says a desktop app cannot keep, comes from the build (`ODDBORO_GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`) and never sits in the repo. Google's hosted MCP servers were measured on 2026-11-07 and refuse tool calls outside its Workspace Developer Preview, so the office's tools take Google's tool names, and moving to Google's servers later is a connector change. The privacy policy says what goes where, including that what an Odd reads goes to the AI service it runs on, and that Windows and Linux keep sign-ins in a shared store. Supersedes the 2026-11-07 Gmail and Drive ruling, which superseded the 2026-11-02 ruling that nothing Google ships until New Altitude has its own client. `markBaseline()` in `packages/ledger/src/upgrade.ts` still takes the old connector's rows out of the one install that had them.
