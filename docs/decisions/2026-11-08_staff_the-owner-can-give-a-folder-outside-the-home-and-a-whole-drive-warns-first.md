- [staff] **Decision (2026-11-08): the owner can give an Odd a folder anywhere on the computer, not only in the home; a folder that would let an Odd past the office's walls is refused with no way around it, and a whole drive or system files warns first with Accept and Cancel.** Owners keep projects on other drives (a Windows owner's work on `D:`), and refusing every folder outside the home blocked them with a line that read as if they had picked the home itself. Refused, whatever the owner says: the home itself, a folder in or holding a secret store (`SECRET_STORES` at the home's top, by name; anywhere else, in the home or outside it, every store in the same list, `STORE_MARKS`: by name, or for a name a project or an ordinary folder may share, by what marks the real store, in the folder, the folders above it and one level down, never deeper, so no walk of a drive; a store under another name is not found, and a `.ssh` with only custom-named keys and no `known_hosts` is given below the home's top), the office's home and Oddboro's code, each of which would let an Odd reach sign-ins or change its own access. A project's own `.claude` or `.codex` of settings is no store. Warned (`broadFolder`): a drive or volume root (`D:\`, `/Volumes/<disk>`, `/mnt/<disk>`, `/media/<user>/<disk>`) or a folder in or holding the system's own folders; a folder in the home never warns. The warning (`FOLDER_WARNING`) shows with a warning icon, a More info link to oddboro.com/info/folder-sharing/, and Accept and Cancel; only Accept, sent as `accept`, gives it, on the Folders tab and on a folder ask's Give access alike. An Odd's `ask_for_folder` never gets one (`FOLDER_REFUSED.broad`), since only the owner may give such a folder. A run's deny rules still name the home's stores only, so a store deeper in a given folder, in the home or outside it, is readable; the site's page tells the owner to check. Supersedes the 2026-11-04 ruling where it refused every folder outside the home, and this ruling's first form where it looked below the home's top by name alone. Code in the ledger's `office-folders.ts` (`checkFolder`), the office's `FolderWarning.svelte`, and the site's `src/content/info/folder-sharing.md`.
