- [office] **Decision (2026-11-12): everything waiting on the owner is one list, Waiting (the Desk's window): asks (Needs your yes), tasks only the owner can do (Needs your hands) and reports (To read). With more than one item, a Next card on top names the first that can be done now, and what it unblocks; the rest are grouped by the goal whose work raised them, then Other work. Within a group, what can be done now comes first, then what unblocks the most, then the oldest, and an item that waits on another open one is dimmed and says so. A waiting Odd still stands outside the owner's door.** A list sorted by time gave a new owner no way to tell where to start, and the owner ruled the order by what waits on what. Approve and deny each take an optional reason, and every decision and finished task goes in the ledger, which credits the owner with their own work. Supersedes the 2026-10-30 ruling on one desk.
