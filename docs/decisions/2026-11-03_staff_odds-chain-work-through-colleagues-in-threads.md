- [staff] **Decision (2026-11-03): an Odd hands a colleague a piece of work with `hand_off`, which opens a thread it leads as the hub. One goal is one thread, led by the manager (or the Odd opening it when the office has none): the goal's planning, its plan and every hand-off for it go there, so a hire serving the goal joins it as a colleague, and an Odd that serves a goal and has no job leads no thread. The colleague starts at once in a fresh run, works at Draft at most and cannot hand on. Its result goes back to the hub, whose pick-up, also at Draft at most, takes the next step: hand on in the same thread, park it until a time or a named task with `resume_at`, ask the owner, or finish. Only the lead says what comes next. A round of work on a thread takes up to 8 hand-offs; the next one waits on the ask whether the work keeps going, a yes starts it as the first of another round and a no stops the thread (`packages/ledger/CLAUDE.md`). Odds do not chat with each other.** Goal plans split one goal across Odds, but nothing passed one Odd's work to the next, so the owner relayed it by hand. The owner wants the Odds to choose the path from a few primitives. Each step costs one run and needs no conversation, polling or standing timer: a colleague finishing, the hub's own time (through the office clock) or the owner's answer moves a thread on. Hub and spoke means no loops or nesting, the limit bounds a round, and the Draft cap means a colleague's words never stand in for the owner's yes. Files cross in the thread's folder, `threads/<id>/<odd>/` in the office's home: every run on the thread reads all of it and writes only its own part, never `CLAUDE.md`, `CLAUDE.local.md` or `.claude`. `hand_off` also copies in files the sender names and can read (owner, 2026-11-09). An ask or task raised on a thread or hand-off carries it, so the owner's answer, or the next result, goes back to it. The hub's chat shows one card per thread, and each goal in the Goals window lists its threads, both by title, with no thread numbers, hand-off counts or tokens. Every goal plan goes on its goal's thread, whatever run raised it, such as a chat or the weekly review; approving it wakes the lead only once its jobs, hire asks and owner task exist, told to lead the work by handing each piece on, following the plan the owner approved, which every run of the lead's on the thread is told with what still waits on the owner; there is no coded stage order. Each hire the plan proposed is linked to its goal in the step that approves it, and wakes the lead again once hired, to hand it its first piece. Hires that land while the lead still runs there wake it once. A colleague handed work on a goal's thread is told the goal's title, why and done-when in its brief. A lead in a chat or a job sees its latest threads, may continue one, and names the one an ask or task is for, or leaves it out for none; a done or failed one reopens with its folder and history for a new round, as it does when an ask or task lands on it, and a stopped one stays stopped, which only a new plan for its goal opens again. Supersedes the 2026-11-02 ruling that work goes one hop and files cross by copy, and the 2026-11-03 rulings that a no-job hire's first day leads a thread of its own for its goal (one goal, one thread), that each round starts with the hub's own estimate (cut 2026-11-04), and that a lead must name a thread or null (owner, 2026-11-11).
