- [staff] **Decision (2026-11-11): a required persona judgment finishes only when the persona answers.** Completed preparation is kept by request in `persona_consultations`. Retry of the same chat or follow-up calls only the persona. A refused or failed judgment keeps its cause and cannot retain the preparation's `Outcome: done`. A caller can explicitly declare optional commentary when the ordinary work itself is the required result. Preparation interrupted before its report was saved is uncertain and is not repeated automatically. `persona-run.ts` composes the result; `persona-consultations.ts` keeps it; the existing chat and follow-up handlers show its failure.
